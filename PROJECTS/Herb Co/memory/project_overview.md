# project_overview

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-01

**Summary:** Webapper is the technical team for Monterey Bay Herb Co. ("Herbco" / NP Nutra), a Beckway portfolio company. The business runs on **iBizFusion** — a custom ColdFusion / MySQL ERP built over 20+ years by a single external developer (Adrian, Romania). What began as a time-boxed audit/stabilize/support engagement has expanded into a retained technical partnership. Jira key `HERB`.

## Why this matters

iBiz is both a strategic asset (automates complex compliance/operations workflows the business depends on) and a legacy liability (single-developer dependency, historically end-of-life stack, security debt). Our job is to stabilize and document it, stand up modern hosting and SDLC, and support the business — leaving iBiz in a state that makes any future replatform easier, not harder.

## What's true today (July 2026)

- iBiz stack upgrade complete; initial security remediation (HERB-1) largely closed.
- **Active:** Monterey Web Consolidation (AWS) + Commerce Migration (Azure) — `HERB-129`, confirmed and kicked off late June 2026, ~September cutover target; SharePoint/M365 migration (`HERB-121`); retained ERP support + Azure/AWS monitoring.
- Anchor client — the relationship is a foothold for further Beckway-portfolio work.
